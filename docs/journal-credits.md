# Chauapédia: content and photographic credits

Reviewed 13 November 2026. The reader uses no gameplay atlas or food thumbnail.

Both photographs depict *Amazona rhodocorytha*, photographed by **Duncan
Rawlinson** at Rare Species Conservatory Foundation, USA, 27 January 2007.
They show a captive bird and are explicitly labelled as species illustrations,
not field photographs of Brazilian habitat. They were resized and converted
to WebP, without altering anatomy or colors. License: **CC BY 2.0**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/ . Attribution, original pages,
license and transformation notice are present in the reader.

- `chaua-portrait.webp`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazona_rhodocorytha_-RSCF-8a.jpg
- `chaua-profile.webp`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazona_rhodocorytha_-RSCF-4e.jpg

Current **Brazilian** status: Vulnerable (VU), Portaria MMA 1.704/2026:
https://www.gov.br/icmbio/pt-br/assuntos/biodiversidade/pan/saiba-mais/documentos-e-downloads/10-portaria-mma-no-1-704-de-16-de-junho-de-2026.pdf

Species assessment, ecology and population estimate: Silveira et al. 2023,
ICMBio/SALVE, https://doi.org/10.37002/salve.ficha.24276.2 . The estimate of
4,607 mature birds is labelled by assessment year, never as a live census.
No global IUCN category is inferred from the Brazilian category.

The INMA 2025 report supports the field observation involving chauás and
*Handroanthus riodocensis*; food resources are not a captive-feeding prescription.
All discovery IDs and collection records are retained.
